Helpful information

Before your visit.

Where is Kaya VIP Thai Spa located?

Kaya VIP Thai Spa is located on the 17th floor of The Metropolis Tower in Business Bay, Dubai, UAE.

What treatments are available?

The listed services include Traditional Thai Massage, Deep Tissue Massage, Aromatherapy Oil Massage, Swedish Relaxation Massage, Moroccan Bath, Hot Stone Massage, Hot Oil Massage and Four Hand Massage. Contact the spa to confirm current availability.

How long are the massage sessions?

The website lists durations of 60–90 minutes for its massage treatments. Please confirm the duration of your chosen service when enquiring.

How can I check prices and availability?

Contact the team by phone or WhatsApp for current prices, available appointment times and treatment details.

Can I ask which treatment is suitable for me?

Yes. Contact the spa before booking to discuss your preferences, desired pressure and any questions about treatment suitability. Share relevant health concerns with the spa team before your session.
